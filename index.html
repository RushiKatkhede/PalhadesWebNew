Who we are

Organised ground transport for businesses that can't afford a late start.

Palhade's Travelling & Multi Services started in Pune in 2007 with one goal: a safe, reliable way to move people and goods.

Founded by Mr. Sandeep, the company set out to bring structure to an industry that had very little of it. Today we manage long- and short-term contracts for corporates, hotels, hospitals and e-commerce networks, with uniform processes across every hub.

Transport is our core. Around it we offer the people who keep it running: trained drivers, delivery partners and security staff, all on one contract.

Daily supply & logistics

How a day runs with Palhade's.

Every contract follows the same four steps at every hub, so you always know where your vehicles are and what you're paying for.

  1. The evening before

    Plan

    Your hub confirms tomorrow's vehicle count and assigns drivers to each route.

  2. Reporting time

    Report

    Vehicles check in at your site. A no-show is flagged and replaced before it costs you a shift.

  3. On the road

    Track

    Live trip status, loads and returns, with OTP-verified deliveries.

  4. End of day

    Sign off & bill

    Each vehicle's day is signed off and flows straight into a clear monthly invoice.

FAQ

Questions clients ask first.

Something else on your mind? Call us on +91 7788 91 7788.

Can I combine transport, drivers and security in one contract?

Yes. Many clients take two or more services from us under a single agreement, so there is one vendor and one point of accountability.

What contract types do you offer?

Yearly and long-term contracts, monthly packages, on-call duties and outstation trips. We shape each one around your shifts, routes and volumes.

Which cities do you serve?

We are based in Pune and run services for clients in Mumbai, Hyderabad, Raipur, Bhopal, Kota and Indore, with pan-India coverage on request.

What happens if a vehicle doesn't report?

Our 24×7 desk flags the no-show and arranges a replacement, so your shift or delivery run isn't left waiting.

Are you a registered company?

Yes. We hold GST, MSME (Udyam), Tours & Travels, Labour and Transport Shop Act registrations. The numbers are listed above.
